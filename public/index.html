作品

The Work

Traditional composition and body flow, carried with modern elements and contemporary design. Each piece is drawn to the individual it will live on.

二

Composition before ink.

The study is timeless composition, body flow, and the aesthetic of traditional Irezumi — then modern art and personal style are worked into it. The design answers to the body it is going on, not to a flash sheet.

三

Crafted to gain from each individual.

Large-scale work is built to accentuate the person wearing it. The same subject placed on two different bodies is two different tattoos — and it is drawn that way.

四

Made to age.

Scale is what lets a tattoo age gracefully. Line weight, spacing and contrast are chosen for how the piece will read in decades — not for how it photographs the week it heals.

経年

Healed

Fresh ink photographs well. Healed ink tells the truth. These are pieces after they settled — which is the only way to judge whether a tattoo was built to last.

彫師

My name is Dylan Prorok.

I'm a tattoo artist based in sunny Las Vegas, Nevada. I follow a traditional Japanese style called Irezumi while incorporating modern elements and contemporary design inspiration. I'm also a painter and muralist — I work in many mediums, and make DIY merchandise by hand.

Outside of art and tattooing, I'm a family man. I've been with my wife since I was nineteen; we married in 2019 and had our first son, Ronin, in 2023. I'm blessed to provide for my family through tattooing full-time.

I've always had a passion for bringing a creative idea to fruition and seeing the tangible product. On skin, that product walks around for the rest of someone's life. This is my Craft, and I hope I will be the one to help you share your Story in ink.
