作品

The Work

Dylan Prorok creates Japanese tattoos at Heritage Tattoo in Las Vegas. Traditional composition crafted to individual body flow, carried with timeless elements and contemporary themes. Each piece is made custom to the individual it will live on.

二

Composition before ink

Each piece begins with a picture and tracing of the body part where the tattoo will be placed, so the tattoo can be designed to fit your body’s natural flow.

三

Crafted to gain from each individual

My aim is to design the tattoo to fit the body as if you were born with the tattoo. The tattoo is built to accentuate the person wearing it.

四

Made to age

Scale and precision are what let a tattoo age gracefully. Line weight, space to breathe, and contrast are chosen strategically so the tattoo can age gracefully in the decades to come.

工程

What a large project is

A large-scale piece is a relationship measured in consistent sessions. Here is an overview of what that looks like before you commit.

一

Inquiry

You send placement, rough size, budget range and any reference. Not a deposit — a conversation starter.

二

Consultation

Talk through your vision, references and placement. Meet virtually for free in 15 minutes, or at Heritage Tattoo for one hour and $200. That $200 goes toward your first session.

三

Drawing

The piece is drawn for you specifically. A $200 non-refundable deposit holds the date and goes toward the total price of your tattoo at the final session. Read the deposit policy.

四

Sessions

Large-scale work is built through consistency. I encourage clients to commit to one session each month, each session being anywhere from 4–8 hours. From experience, this is the best route to completing a large-scale project in a timely manner that allows the tattoo to age evenly.

五

Healing & wear

After each appointment I will provide aftercare instructions. I offer free touch-ups on my work for up to 6 months after the last appointment.
