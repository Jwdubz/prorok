My name is Dylan Prorok.

I’ve been tattooing in Las Vegas for nine years. Born in Honolulu and raised in Las Vegas, my Japanese heritage and upbringing in Hawaii sparked a lifelong appreciation for Japanese art and culture. As a teenager in Las Vegas, I immersed myself in hardcore, skateboarding, graffiti, and the underground culture surrounding tattooing. Tattooing became the place where all of those influences naturally converged.

As a half-Japanese artist, I’ve always felt a strong connection to the timeless tradition of irezumi. While I enjoy exploring other styles, Japanese tattooing remains at the heart of my work. My goal is to create tattoos that feel authentic to each client—pieces that flow naturally with the body, complement its composition, and look as though they were meant to be there from the beginning.

I believe great tattoos should carry strength, character, and purpose while transcending trends and standing the test of time. Just as important is the relationship behind the work: building trust, understanding each client’s vision, and creating connections that often grow into genuine, lasting friendships.
