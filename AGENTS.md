# Dylan Prorok website rules

- Do not add or restore decorative particle overlays anywhere on this site. This includes falling or floating dots, petals, snow, confetti, sparks, and equivalent effects under different names.
- This rule applies on desktop and mobile, including full-motion mode. Restoring motion must never restore the old particle canvas or simulation.
- Preserve the approved photographs, videos, reveals, and smooth scrolling. The particle prohibition is not a request to disable those effects.
- Keep the no-particle regression check in scripts/validate-site.py active.
